Copy to `source-data/skills.md` and replace with your real data.

This file is the **allowlist** for tools. The Resume Writer may not name a tool
that is absent here, and the Fact Checker treats any such mention as a finding.

Every skill must cite the EXP or PRJ that proves it. A skill with no proving
source is not a skill you can put on a tailored resume — move it to 학습 중.

## 숙련도 기준

Be conservative. Over-stating here is how an unsupported claim enters the resume
through a path the Fact Checker cannot catch — it verifies against this file, so
this file's honesty is not verifiable by the workflow.

| 수준 | 기준 |
|---|---|
| 상 | 프로덕션에서 주도적으로 사용, 장애 대응 및 설계 경험 있음 |
| 중 | 프로덕션에서 사용했으나 주도적 설계 경험은 없음 |
